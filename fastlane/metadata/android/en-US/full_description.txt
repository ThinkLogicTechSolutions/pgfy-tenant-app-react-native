Fitrivo is the all-in-one app for production crews and partners. Stay on top of your projects, schedules, and callsheets from your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Projects & assignments
View and manage your assigned projects in one place. See project details and jump straight into schedules and scene information.

• Schedules & callsheets
Access today’s and weekly schedules. Open callsheet details, see who’s on the crew, and keep everything in sync.

• Scripts & scene details
Read project scripts and scene details in-app. Download scripts as PDF for offline use and open them in your preferred viewer.

• Weather on location
Check weather for your schedule or scene location so you can plan ahead.

• Notifications
Get notified about updates so you don’t miss important changes to schedules or projects.

• Help & support
Submit queries with optional attachments, browse FAQs, and get help when you need it.

• Safety bulletins
View safety bulletins and stay informed on set.

• Profile & account
Manage your account details and keep your information up to date.

Built for crew members and production partners who need quick access to projects, schedules, scripts, and team info—Fitrivo keeps everything in one place.
